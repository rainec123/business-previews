Right on the Sand

Where Narrow Neck Beach meets your morning ritual

You'll find us where the promenade meets the shoreline — the spot where morning runners stop for their post-workout smoothie bowl, where parents grab a long black while the kids chase waves, and where the regulars know exactly which table catches the best morning light.

We're not trying to be fancy. We're here because Narrow Neck deserved a proper cafe — one that understands the difference between a rushed takeaway and a slow Saturday morning with the paper. Our kitchen opens early because the surf crowd needs fuel, and we stay open late because sunset over Rangitoto doesn't wait for anyone.

The coffee is strong, the cabinet food is made fresh each morning, and the view is unbeatable. That's the formula. That's why you'll see the same faces here every week — because once you find your spot on this beach, you keep coming back.
