Welcome to Narrow Neck Beach Cafe

Nestled in one of Auckland's most picturesque beachside locations, Narrow Neck Beach Cafe is your go-to destination for quality coffee, fresh food, and genuine hospitality. We're passionate about creating a warm, welcoming atmosphere where locals and visitors alike can relax, connect, and enjoy the best the beach has to offer.

Whether you're starting your day with a perfectly crafted espresso, grabbing lunch with a view, or catching up with friends over a sweet treat, we've got you covered. Our team takes pride in every cup we pour and every plate we serve. We believe that great coffee and good company are the foundation of a perfect day at the beach.

We're committed to providing exceptional service and creating memories that last. From early morning surfers to afternoon beach-goers, Narrow Neck Beach Cafe is where the community gathers. Come see why we're Auckland's favourite beachside cafe.
