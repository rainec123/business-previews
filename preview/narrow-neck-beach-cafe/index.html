Right on the Sand, Right on Time

We're the cafe that sits on Narrow Neck Beach with our toes practically in the Waitematā Harbour. You know us — the one where the windows fog up on cold mornings and the deck fills with golden retrievers and salty wetsuits by 8am.

Our regulars are the swimmers who brave the winter water, the mums with prams doing the beach loop, the Takapuna workers who detour for a proper coffee before the day starts. We're open early because we know you need us early.

The menu is what you'd hope for: açaí bowls loaded with local fruit, bacon and egg rolls that actually satisfy, cabinet food that changes daily based on what our baker feels like making. And the flat whites? Strong, silky, consistent. The kind you think about on your commute.

This is the spot where Devonport feels like a village. Where you run into your neighbour, where the barista knows your order, where the view makes you remember why you live in Auckland.

What the Regulars Order

☕

The House Flat White

Our single-origin blend, pulled tight and poured smooth. This is the one the 7am crowd can't start without — bold, balanced, no bitterness.

🥑

Smashed Avo on Sourdough

Creamy avocado, heirloom tomatoes, feta crumble, and a crack of good salt on thick-cut sourdough from the bakery down the road. Simple, perfect, always fresh.

🍓

Açaí Bowl

Thick açaí base topped with granola, fresh berries, banana, coconut, and a drizzle of local honey. The post-swim reward that tastes like summer.

🥓

Bacon & Egg Roll

Crispy streaky bacon, fried egg with a runny yolk, rocket, aioli, on a soft brioche bun. Messy, satisfying, worth every napkin.

🥐

Almond Croissant

Flaky, buttery, filled with almond cream and dusted with icing sugar. Baked fresh every morning and gone by 10am most days.

🍰

Cabinet Slice of the Day

Our baker's mood dictates the lineup — lemon curd tart, carrot cake, chocolate brownie, banana bread. Always homemade, always worth the calories.
