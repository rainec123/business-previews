Our Story

Where Takapuna Comes to Wake Up Right

Kinship isn't trying to reinvent coffee — we're just doing it properly. Our beans are sourced from specialty roasters, our baristas know the difference between a good shot and a great one, and our kitchen turns out brunch food that's actually worth getting out of bed for. The regulars come back because we're consistent, welcoming, and we don't pretend to be anything we're not.

We opened because Takapuna deserved a cafe that cared about the details without being precious about it. That means properly steamed milk, avocado toast that doesn't cost a fortune, and eggs benedict that arrives hot. Our cold brew is steeped for 18 hours because shortcuts show. Our acai bowls are loaded with fresh fruit because anything less feels lazy.

The best part? Walk in on a Tuesday morning and you'll see the same faces — the early swimmers from the beach, the office workers grabbing their daily fix, the parents with prams who've made this their ritual. It's the kind of place where the staff know your name by your third visit, and that's exactly how we like it.
