Our Story

Where Specialty Beans Meet North Shore Mornings

We opened Kinship because Takapuna deserved a coffee spot that takes the beans as seriously as the welcome. Just off the main drag, close enough to the beach that you can taste the salt air with your cold brew, we've become the place locals duck into before work and linger in on weekends. Our baristas know your order by your second visit.

The specialty beans we source aren't here to show off — they're here because great coffee starts with great raw material, roasted right, pulled with care. Our brunch menu follows the same logic: simple things done well. Avocado toast that doesn't need a filter, eggs benedict with hollandaise we make every morning, acai bowls for the post-swim crowd.

What keeps people coming back isn't just the coffee, though plenty have called it the best in Takapuna. It's the feeling of a place that knows what it is. Warm without trying too hard. Skilled without being precious. The kind of spot where you can read the paper in peace or catch up with a friend, and either way, you'll leave better than you arrived.
